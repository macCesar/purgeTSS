A single SVG is all Purge T S S needs to brand a Titanium Classic project.

From the integrated terminal, run: Purge T S S brand, dash dash yes.

The command creates every branding asset for the enabled iOS and Android platforms.

The iOS splash set now rounds only the artwork, while the master icon stays square.

Android applies the same radius to its main launch image and every density and orientation variant.

Feature Graphic gets the same rounded artwork. Store and launcher icons stay square for platform masking. That is Purge T S S: one logo, one command, and every branding asset.
